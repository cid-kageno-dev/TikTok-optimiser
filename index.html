2. Lossless container remux

Remuxing copies the existing video/audio streams into a fresh MP4 container without re-encoding. This can fix some container/metadata issues, but it cannot create real 4K detail or 60 fps, increase the source bitrate, or force TikTok to serve 4K/60 fps.

The remux engine is loaded on demand from a public CDN and requires an internet connection. Browser memory limits may prevent processing large videos on phones.

What “4K/60 fps” means

  • Portrait 4K is typically 2160 × 3840; landscape 4K is 3840 × 2160.
  • Bitrate shown below is an estimate from file size ÷ duration, not the exact video-stream bitrate.
  • Browser playback metadata does not reliably expose frame rate for every file. This page labels FPS as unverified unless it can be detected.
  • TikTok may transcode uploads and deliver a lower resolution or frame rate.
Privacy note: this page does not upload your selected videos to a backend. If you choose remux, the browser downloads the FFmpeg engine from jsDelivr. For private files, use a trusted browser and avoid sharing generated videos publicly.
